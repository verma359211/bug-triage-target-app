# Business rules

- Combined percentage discounts cannot exceed 50%.
- Money is represented as integer cents.
- Tax is 8.25% and is rounded half up once on the discounted subtotal.
- A customer cannot order more units than are currently in stock.
- Shipping is free when the discounted subtotal is at least $50.00.
- Standard shipping costs $7.99.
- Checkout requires at least one cart item.
